# Admit actual auxiliary reference data

This preparation work supplies the requested early supervision; it does not
replace affinity, processing, MS or TCR outcome training. No auxiliary-quality
or transfer claim follows from a catalog census or successful software tests.

1. Pin actual MHC catalog, Weirdo reference/splits and source-building code.
   Census raw class/species/chain, sequence availability and quality flags before
   selecting vocabulary or cohorts. Record the actual paths, hashes and source
   semantics. A class-II chain catalog is not an observed alpha/beta pairing
   dataset. Do not impute missing partners to make assay observations.
2. For a prospectively fixed, label-blind Weirdo subset, audit every selected
   peptide's category membership and source-protein occurrences against the
   pinned reference. Verify exclusion of cross-split occurrences, canonical
   residues, duplicate keys and source split/bucket reconstruction. Existing
   table-verification disagreement needs explanation, not silently relabeled
   outcomes. The stored 20 buckets and mnemonic-prefix family keys are source
   grouping devices; neither proves homology-based biological independence.
3. Audit MHC sequence aliases, identical-sequence label ambiguity and split
   clusters. Preserve individual-chain annotation evidence independently from
   genuine paired inputs. If the observation/model contract cannot consume an
   available single-chain annotation without inventing a partner, extend that
   auxiliary-only contract explicitly before preparing records. Pair compatibility
   labels require their own observed or explicitly generated reference evidence.
4. Freeze class/species/source vocabularies, applicability/length domains,
   source- and sequence-group partitions and dispositions before a bounded
   registered pretraining recipe. Report majority/composition baselines,
   per-label accuracy/discrimination and calibration, confidence coverage/error,
   unseen-domain behavior and subsequent assay transfer separately. Species
   catalog performance cannot be inferred from human-only affinity data.

TCR reference/pairing and phenotyped CD4/CD8 data need a separate source census.
Author access to TCRvdb remains pending; no exact source adapter may be inferred
without its authorized schema. Actual source cells, full versus reconstructed
chains and receptor pairing must remain visible in that preparation contract.

The initial inventory is data-only, with raw counts and hashes. Before model
training or evaluation, freeze the specific bounded recipe and register it in
the canonical experiment workflow. This census supplies no performance evidence.
