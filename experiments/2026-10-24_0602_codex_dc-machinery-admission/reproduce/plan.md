## Candidate and evidence scope

Primary source: de Verteuil et al. 2010, PMID20484733, PMC2938112,
DOI10.1074/mcp.M900566-MCP200. Upstream owner:
https://github.com/pirl-unc/hitlist/issues/798 (verifiedopen), extending broadmouse13.
The primary article identifies DC peptide measurements under explicit machinery
loss and specificity controls. No original quantitative asset/schema has yet been
admitted; the issue's summary is not a data-ready claim. Source biological findings
have been read, so any future evaluation is literature-informed, not a blind study.

This source supplies an independent APC population and explicit perturbation
metadata. It does not eliminate MHC selection, transcript/precursor changes,
measurement effects or dependence. Do not relabel RNA as protein abundance,
gene loss as a measured catalytic rate, or absence as numeric zero.

spec from actual admitted schema/support. Separate source ratios from detection
events and bounds; account for observation/control sampling, population/specimen
dependencies and all precursor alternatives. Use a parsimonious identified endpoint
with condition/input controls and an exact physical reference. Allocate only
supported parameters; predeclare split, weights, transforms, initialization,
budget, qualifiers and a meaningful biological improvement gate.

Keep the known failures as fixed context. Do not claim a newly frozen split alone
establishes external independence from previous native or mouse sources; measure
sequence/parent/study overlap and state unresolved homology. Consider using this
source for prospective external transfer only when its inputs can be supplied
without outcome fitting; a new cohort fit must be labeled accordingly.

Compare a sampled/discrete training method only under a clearly matched objective
and when the exact model supplies useful biological signal or the observation
objective itself requires sampling. Preserve exact forward/conditional hybrids as
hybrids. Do not substitute a numerical gradient success for distribution/response
recovery. No claim of free-peptide concentrations from an elution-only endpoint.

hashes, original-field/lineage replay and clear trainability limits. Then advance
to the preregistered biological comparison, rather than another broad source audit.
