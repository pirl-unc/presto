# DC source sequence/length/coordinate forensic check

The structural source audit finds every DC literal sequence one residue longer than reported Length, with most matched deposited parent occurrences starting one residue before reported Start and ending at reported End. Do not silently remove a residue or train on an assumed correction.

Before computing new results, compare source Mass with neutral monoisotopic masses for (a) the complete literal sequence plus explicit numeric inline deltas and (b) the same string with exactly its first residue and any immediately attached delta removed. These are source-annotation hypotheses, not generated biological training candidates. Independently compare unmodified masses from installed Pyteomics and Biopython to0.002Da; preserve all source values and both residuals. A descriptive source-mass match requires absolute residual<=0.1Da to accommodate source delta rounding; no threshold changes after counts. Report per-block counts and every row, not just favorable examples. Skip unsupported source notation explicitly.

Compare the two literal sequences against original deposited PeptideEvidence sequence alternatives as additional source evidence; shared sequence presence is not a one-to-one quantity/acquisition match. Do not claim confirmed real peptide identity from mass alone. This is a metadata hypothesis check, not training, predictor evaluation, source mutation, eligibility selection or test reuse. Preserve original canonical source flags and submit findings to Hitlist817. Any eventual canonical sequence-resolution rule requires its own explicit provenance, source-original preservation and tests before a recipe is admitted.
