## Question and relation to the full goal

Can PXD058121 / DOI10.1073/pnas.2419607122 supply original substrate/product
quantity trajectories that constrain generation and subsequent destruction of
short and long peptides? This could provide an independent kinetic constraint
for the availability model before cellular transfer. Purified organ-derived20S
activity is not intact-cell APC concentration. Organ labels are not purified
cell identities, and they do not determine catalytic-subunit composition without
measurements. One substrate does not establish sequence-generalization.

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11874349/ . The paper
names five original XLSX datasets, an SI PDF and PRIDE PXD058121. It reports
alpha-synuclein time-course products including short fragments and intact substrate;
original file schemas, per-replicate quantities and preparation mappings remain
unqualified. Source claims are hypotheses to check, not admitted training labels.

## Qualification sequence

1. Freeze original publication XML/metadata, all listed supplementary asset URLs,
PRIDE project/file inventory and source rights/access status. Retrieve only public
processed tables/metadata and SI needed to identify units, assignment/filtering,
substrate constructs, organ/species/preparation, time, control, sample and replicate
semantics. No RAW spectra or instrument reprocessing. Preserve failed HTTP bodies
and distinguish access errors from absence; no author outreach or access bypass.
2. Inventory all workbook cells/sheets using read-only independent parsers; preserve
original values, coordinates, raw identifiers, formulas versus cached results,
header structure and missingness. Confirm any original values against a second
OOXML reader before quantitative admission. Peptide sequence, position, mass,
charge, molecular form, ambiguity, assignment method, confidence/fit factor and
replicate recurrence filters are independent provenance dimensions.
3. Establish reported intensity/area/abundance definitions, normalizations,
replicate granularity, time-zero processing, control background and actual input
concentrations. A known substrate input does not calibrate every peptide's MS
response. Preserve multiply assigned masses and unassigned signals; do not select
an assignment by modeled propensity, add pseudocounts, infer missing negatives,
invent detection bounds or equate molecular signal with molarity.
4. If quantitative trajectories with source-backed identities are available, file
or extend a Hitlist issue with original asset inventory and a homogeneous typed
source/recipe specification. Shared canonical ingestion and original-value replay
must precede fitting. Presto may audit original cells, not become a private source
curator. Otherwise preserve the exact missing quantities/context and stop this
source family; do not substitute counts/plots for quantitative observations.
5. Freeze a separate biological experiment only after admission. Candidate next
comparison is a minimal generation/destruction model versus direct time controls,
with matched exact and sampled gradients and support/identifiability gates. Do not
choose model parameters, thresholds or splits from source audit outcomes. Holdout
scope must follow actual independent substrates/preparations; a single substrate
cannot support a new-protein generalization claim.
