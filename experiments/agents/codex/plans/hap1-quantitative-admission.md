## Predictive decision

Preregister a separate comparison only after inspecting available actual schema.
Use measured responses and simple condition/input controls; retain detection versus
conditional-abundance endpoints separately. Hold out connected source-protein/
peptide components; evaluate unseen perturbations only if the training data really
identify their machinery representation. No rerun of inspected native/APC/MAPP tests.
No inference of independent gene effects from one clone/KO alone.

Exact differentiation remains the fixed practical reference. Compare sampled
training under the identical objective when the exact model shows meaningful
held-out signal or a set-level observation objective requires it. No sampler work
as a substitute for biological failure. Account for MHC selection and observation
confounding; elution alone cannot validate intracellular concentrations.

This admission family has no fitting, split, pretraining, synthetic data or predictive
metrics. Preserve a full reproducibility bundle and close all source findings in
the canonical registry; then advance to a registered biological comparison.
