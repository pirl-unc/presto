## Why the endpoint must change

For a peptide p measured under the same response function in two conditions,
I(p,c)=D(p)*A(p,c). Thus log(I(p,treated)/I(p,control)) equals the log amount ratio:
the sequence-only D(p) cancels. The newly frozen observer cannot improve HAP1 or
macrophage same-peptide fold-change predictions by being added to both sides.
If acquisition or matrix changes D, a condition-dependent response model would
need additional independent supervision; do not invent it from the desired answer.

The next meaningful transfer endpoint is relative peptide signal **within** a
condition/parent, where peptide-specific response affects the observed composition.
Even there, MHC elution also depends on selection and capture, and conditional
positive-set composition does not identify mass outside the observed set.
