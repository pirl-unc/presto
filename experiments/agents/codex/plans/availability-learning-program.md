October 22, 2026; Codex / GPT-6. The user's objective is a working, validated
model of likely peptide concentrations in antigen-presenting cells conditional
on precursor abundance, using focused comparisons of differentiable and sampled
learning. Algorithm demonstrations do not close that objective.

Preserve the original checkout and use the existing modular-model worktree.
Previous goal work produced verified synthetic distribution fits; the intervening

## Required work

1. A registered factorial test of clipping, group standardization, sample reuse
   and reference KL, retaining exact and original REINFORCE references. Include
   partially enumerated gradients, gradient fidelity and actual work accounting.
2. A separate registered comparison under finite partial observations and
   multiset-level objectives. Distinguish latent recovery from fitting the
   observation distribution; preserve counts and repeated draws. Use exactly
   tractable synthetic controls to detect biased objectives/estimators.
3. Audit real endogenous biological measurements for source sequence/coordinates,
   specimen identity, precursor abundance, peptide amount or intensity, units,
   sampling scope, observation bias and censoring. Prefer source-linked
   quantitative datasets with independent validation. Do not relabel ligand
   detection, raw MS intensity or synthetic rewards as cellular concentration.
4. Register biological comparisons only after defining the identified endpoint,
   matched baseline, holdout units and candidate-support contract. Test a narrow
   scientific hypothesis at a time. Retain complete raw lineage and predictions.
5. Integrate only demonstrated components into the canonical model and validate
   relative availability and supported concentration claims against held-out
   measurements. Unknown calibration, compartment, residence time and precursor
   abundance remain explicit limitations rather than implicit defaults.

## Completion criteria

The objective stays open until actual biological inputs produce independently
validated, nonconstant peptide availability predictions under an explicit
precursor-normalization and quantity contract. All requested algorithm choices,
partial observations and multiset/hybrid comparisons must have measured outcomes,
not just proposed code. Source audits or synthetic success are intermediate steps.
