# Prospective kinetic support census

After independent source replay, inspect support for a future kinetic cohort.
This stage is descriptive source qualification, not training, validation, model
selection or a biological performance test. Original package bytes stay frozen.

Use only the deposited F2 kinetic view, avoiding duplicate F1 views and publisher
selection. Illustrative candidates require positive finite assignment intensity,
matching input-mass intensity, a valid substrate span, one reported and replayed
assignment, absolute source mass error at most 10 ppm, and explicit absence of
reported modifications. Any remaining multiple measured masses per sequence and
acquisition remain ambiguous; omit the entire duplicate group rather than choose
by intensity or silently sum. Keep all row dispositions and source identities.

Report exact acquisition/time/replicate counts; peptide support across times and
replicates; intact-substrate coverage; shared sequences between early and late
time ranges. Identify whether a product/substrate ratio can cancel run scaling
and repeated-peptide temporal contrasts can cancel fixed peptide MS response.
Those are possible endpoints, not calibrated concentrations. No missing value
becomes zero, a nonbinder, a censor bound or a negative.

Do not choose a split, minimum-support pass threshold, model or hyperparameter in
this census. A separately registered experiment must declare those before any
fitting or outcome-based selection. One protein/animal only permits narrow
within-source kinetic checks. Preserve every original ambiguity and source gap.
