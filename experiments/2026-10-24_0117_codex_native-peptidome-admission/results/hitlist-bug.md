The real-source audit of merged PR790 (`f4d3a6d0f1933162f9ac6eac42ac1b134c8a27dc`, package 2.0.15) reproduces all 10,564 exported source rows and 61,908 ratio slots, but finds semantic errors that prevent a reliable training recipe. This is a concrete follow-up to #788/#789, not a request for another importer.

## Reproduced problems

1. **Cross-paper matching cannot succeed.** `_compile` includes `source_id` in `measurement_key`, then asks whether one resulting hash contains multiple source IDs. Every one of 61,908 exported lineage records is consequently `source_unique`. An independent comparison finds **830 candidate groups / 1,660 rows** with the same raw cell line, sequence, charge, observed mass and first five ratio values across the two primary workbooks (requiring at least two numeric ratio values). These are possible reused measurements, not proof of specimen identity. Example: Dasgupta S1 row13 and Gelman S1 row1477 share the sequence, observed mass, treatment and all ratios. Both papers explicitly describe reused measurements. Source-specific row IDs must remain unique, while candidate reuse keys must be source-independent; unmatched rows must retain unresolved independence.

2. **Tentative annotations can change sequence identity.** Dasgupta S1 K587 is `(Ac-AGKQAVSASGK)`. The parser returns `AAGKQAVSASGK` and no modification: it strips parentheses after checking the acetyl prefix, then extracts uppercase letters from `Ac-`. The table legend explicitly identifies parenthesized sequences as tentative. Across the package, 276 ratio slots carry parenthesized sequence annotations and **219 are marked `candidate_exact`** solely because their ratios are numeric. Preserve identification confidence separately from numeric exactness; never admit a tentative identity as an unqualified sequence label. Reject/defer unsupported annotation syntax rather than removing arbitrary characters.

3. **Modification information is lost.** There are 540 ratio slots whose source sequence contains `Mox`, defined in the legend as oxidized methionine. For example Dasgupta K142 `Ac-MoxQNDAGEFVDLYVPRK` retains acetylation but loses methionine oxidation in `peptide_modifications_raw`. Other forms include `pyroE-` and a source annotation `APIKVGDAIPAVE -18 (dehydro? - maps to Cterm E)`; uppercase-letter extraction must not append annotation text to a biological sequence. Preserve original text, positions where supported, and explicit unresolved modification status.

4. **Acquisition provenance is assigned incorrectly.** `_context_for` assigns `Waters Synapt G1` to every row. [Dasgupta 2014 Methods](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0103604) name Synapt G2, whereas [Gelman 2013 Methods](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0053263) name Synapt G1. Dasgupta also republishes earlier measurements, so simply assigning G2 to every row in that workbook is insufficient. Distinguish publishing-study protocol assertions from the original acquisition protocol and unresolved row-level inheritance.

5. **Exposure and harvest timing are conflated.** 5,041 primary rows contain a wash annotation in the average-label column. For example Dasgupta B9 says `MG132 35min 2.5 uM` while V9 says `20min+15'wash`; Gelman B10 says `bortezomib 30min, 500nM` while U10 says `10min+wash`. These assertions are preserved in raw row JSON, but the context exports a single `duration_hours` as if it were an unambiguous exposure. Expose separate source assertions / treatment and handling intervals, including conflicts; do not silently choose a duration or convert chemical exposure into genetic loss.

## Acceptance criteria

- Add failing-before regression cases for the real examples above and a two-source reuse fixture. Replay all raw rows/ratios unchanged after correction.
- Keep source-cell identity, candidate-reuse relationships, specimen independence, identification confidence, quantitative exactness and recipe eligibility separate. Avoid declaring unmatched sources independent.
- Preserve supported modifications and every unresolved annotation; unknown/tentative peaks remain queryable but deferred from confident sequence recipes.
- Represent acquisition and exposure provenance through shared catalog/query fields with explicit source scope and overrides/adjudication; no Presto-only repair.
- Audit new counts and provide a canonical recipe contract that excludes derived heatmap averages, avoids treating control ratios / charge states / reused rows as independent biological targets, and reports unresolved dependence.
