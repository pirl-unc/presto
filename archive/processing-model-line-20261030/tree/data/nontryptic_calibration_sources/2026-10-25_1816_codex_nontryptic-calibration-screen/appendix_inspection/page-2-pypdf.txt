SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIALS & METHODS 
 
Cell culture 
SKMEL5 cells were obtained from ATCC [ATCC HTB-70] and maintained in DMEM medium 
(Corning) supplemented with 10% FBS (Gibco) and 1% penicillin/streptomycin (Gibco). Cells were 
routinely tested for mycoplasma contamination, and maintained in 37 ºC, 5% CO2.  
  
Human Tumor HLA typing 
HLA-A2 positivity (Dataset S5) in T1 was confirmed with western blot by probing with an anti-
HLA-A2 antibody (Abcam, ab168405), and confirmed in T2-T10 by HLA typing performed via 
sequencing of patient PBMC-derived DNA. Tumor samples were collected, SNAP frozen, and stored at 
-80C prior to analysis. 
 
Flow cytometry 
Cells were seeded in 10 cm plates, allowed to adhere overnight, and subsequently treated with 
binimetinib (Selleck Chemicals) or DMSO as a vehicle control for 72 hrs in triplicate. Cells were 
harvested with trypsin, washed 1x with ice cold PBS supplemented with 1% FBS and 0.1% sodium 
azide (flow buffer) and incubated with fluorophore-conjugated pan-specific HLA antibody (W6/32, 
Biolegend, cat # 311404) at 0.5 μg mL-1 in flow buffer for 30 minutes on ice. Cells were washed 1x with 
flow buffer and resuspended with propidium iodide. Analyses were performed on an LSRII (BD 
Biosciences) and data analyzed using FlowJo (version 10.6.2), gated as previously described.1  
 
Peptide synthesis 
All peptides were generated using HeavyPeptide AQUA Custom Synthesis Service (Thermo 
Fisher Scientific). Briefly, all peptides were synthesized using the latest Fmoc solid-phase technology. 
Synthesized peptides were cleaved using standard cleavage cocktail and purified to >97% as 
determined by HPLC using reversed phase chromatography (C18, 5um column). Molecular weight was 
confirmed using a mass spectrometer equipped with a nanospray source. Peptide content was 
determined by amino acid analysis and isotopologues were balanced by LC/MS using a Thermo 
Scientific Velos Pro mass spectrometer equipped with a nanoflex source (C18 Acclaim PepMap 100; 
Thermo Fisher Scientific). Heavy isotope labeled amino acids used for synthesis and peptides with 
annotated isotope-labeled amino acids are listed in Dataset S6A.  
 
UV-mediated peptide exchange for hipMHCs 
UV-mediated peptide exchange was performed using recombinant, biotinylated Flex-T HLA-
A*02:01 monomers (BioLegend), using a modified version of the commercial protocol as previously 
described.1 Concentration of stable complexes following peptide exchange was quantified using the 
Flex-T HLA class I ELISA assay (Biolegend) per manufacturer’s instructions for HLA-A*02:01. ELISA 
results were acquired using a Tecan plate reader Infinite 200 with Tecan icontrol version 1.7.1.12.  
 
Peptide MHC isolation

with binimetinib or DMSO. At the time of harvest, cells were washed with 1x PBS, and lifted using 
0.05% Trypsin-EDTA (Gibco). Cells were pelleted at 500 g for 5 minutes, washed twice more in 1x 
PBS, and pelleted again. Cell pellets were resuspended in 1 mL MHC lysis buffer [20 nM Tris-HCl pH
